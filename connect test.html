Connect: feel test

Select two or more sentences and press Ctrl+Shift+/. Connectors are written straight into your text and highlighted. Nothing is saved. Rules only, no AI calls. You can also type or paste your own text.

While applied: Esc undoes, Tab or Enter keeps, Alt+R rerolls, Alt+1–9 toggles a seam, or keep typing to keep it.

Judge:

  1. Does the result still sound like you?
  2. Is one shortcut enough, or did you want to choose the relationship first?
  3. Does editing in place feel better than a preview panel?
