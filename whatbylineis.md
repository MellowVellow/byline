**Byline. Where thoughts find words.**

**Byline is a web rich text editor built on TipTap with FastAPI as a backend. It combines LLM-generated inline suggestions and synonyms, deterministic grammar checks using harper.js, and persona-based reader feedback all in one interface.**

**Byline is a writing tool like Google Docs. It suggests how to continue your sentence, auto-fixes (optionally) mistakes, and tells you what a reader would understand from your text.**
